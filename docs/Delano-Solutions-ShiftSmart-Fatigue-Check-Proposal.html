ShiftSmart Fatigue Reaction Check

A fast, measurable pre-start screening tool for employees and equipment operators — built by Delano Solutions to help organisations reduce fatigue-related risk before work begins.

Executive summary

ShiftSmart is a short, game-based reaction test (about 30 seconds) that operators complete before driving or operating equipment. Staff identify themselves with their clock number; the system records each click’s reaction time and compares results to personal and company baselines. When performance suggests possible fatigue, the platform can flag concern and support supervisor follow-up (alert routing can be configured to your policies).

The problem

Fatigue reduces reaction time, focus, and decision quality — yet it is often hard to see or self-report accurately. Companies face safety, compliance, liability, and operational costs when alertness is impaired at shift start.

The solution

A consistent, digital pre-start check that produces objective data instead of relying only on conversation or visual judgment — without adding significant delay to the sign-on process.

How it works

  1. Operator enters clock number (linked to preloaded name and ID).
  2. Brief on-screen instructions; operator closes the modal when ready.
  3. Countdown 3, 2, 1, then the game runs for ~30 seconds.
  4. Random targets appear; operator taps/clicks each as quickly as possible.
  5. System stores reaction times, misses, and session summary.
  6. Results are compared to personal baseline and company baseline; concerns can be flagged.

Benefits for your organisation

  • Safer pre-start decisions — screen alertness before high-risk tasks.
  • Objective measurement — repeatable reaction-time data across the workforce.
  • Personal baselines — detect when someone is slower than their normal (e.g. starting standard 550 ms until history builds).
  • Company-wide insight — trends and benchmarks for safety reporting.
  • Low friction — ~30 seconds per check; suitable for depots and kiosks.
  • Scalable — designed for large rosters (hundreds of employees per site).
  • Compliance support — documented, consistent fatigue-management process.
Why Delano Solutions? We combine practical field experience with a deployable product: operator identification, session storage, baseline logic, and a clear path to supervisor alerts — so the tool fits real transport and industrial operations, not just a one-off demo.

Recommended deployment

Integrate ShiftSmart into daily sign-on: complete the check before keys or equipment are issued. If results are within range, proceed; if flagged, apply your existing escalation (conversation, rest, reassignment, or stand-down per policy).

Conclusion

ShiftSmart helps organisations act before incidents by turning fatigue risk into measurable, trackable data. Delano Solutions invites you to pilot the system with your employee roster and tailor thresholds, branding, and alert workflows to your safety program.
